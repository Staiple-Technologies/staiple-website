Unbuilt Houses

Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026Applies to the Unbuilt Houses Pinterest app

What this app is

The Unbuilt Houses app is a private publishing tool operated by Staiple Technologies (staiple.com) for its @unbuilthouses studio account. It is run by the account owner, on the owner's own computer, to create pins of the owner's own architectural imagery on the owner's own Pinterest boards. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the account owner.

Information the app accesses

With the owner's consent through Pinterest's authorisation screen, the app can:

  • read the owner's own account name, account id and list of boards;
  • create and update pins on the owner's own boards.

The app does not read, collect or store any information about other Pinterest users, their pins, boards, comments, messages or activity. It does not read Pinterest's audience, advertising or analytics data.

Information the app stores

  • The access and refresh tokens that Pinterest issues for the owner's account, kept in a configuration file on the owner's computer.
  • A local record of the pins the app has created: pin ids, titles and dates.

Nothing is stored on third-party servers. The app sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking of any kind.

How information is shared

The only recipient of any data is Pinterest itself, through its official API, for the purpose of creating pins. No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

Retention and revoking access

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes the app's access from the connected apps section of their Pinterest account settings, after which the app can no longer act on the account. The local record of created pins is kept as the owner's own archive.

Children

The app is not directed at anyone under 18 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the @unbuilthouses account on Pinterest or Instagram.
